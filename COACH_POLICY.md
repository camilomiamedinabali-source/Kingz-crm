# Jess Academy Coach Policy & Guidelines

**Effective Date:** August 17, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026  
**Owner:** Kingz Chess Academy Management

---

## 1. Overview

This document outlines the professional standards, expectations, and guidelines for all coaches at Jess Academy. Coaches are representatives of the academy and must maintain the highest standards of professionalism, integrity, and customer service.

Jess Academy operates three primary service channels:
- **Private Lessons** - One-on-one coaching sessions
- **Marketing Schools** - Group chess programs at partner schools
- **Channels** - Other direct customer engagement and instruction programs

---

## 2. Code of Professional Conduct

### 2.1 General Professional Standards

All coaches must:
- Maintain professional demeanor in all interactions with students, parents, and academy staff
- Be punctual and reliable for all scheduled sessions
- Arrive prepared with lesson plans and materials
- Maintain a clean, professional appearance
- Communicate respectfully and courteously at all times
- Act as ambassadors for Jess Academy's reputation and values

### 2.2 Integrity and Honesty

- Provide accurate progress reports for all students
- Do not misrepresent student skills, achievements, or promotion readiness
- Be honest about availability, capabilities, and limitations
- Do not overstate or exaggerate academic results
- Report incidents, concerns, or issues to management promptly

### 2.3 Confidentiality

- Do not share student information, payment details, or personal data with unauthorized individuals
- Do not discuss one student's progress with other students or parents
- Keep academy business strategies, pricing, and operational details confidential
- Protect academy materials, lesson plans, and intellectual property

---

## 3. Customer Interaction Guidelines

### 3.1 Direct Customer Communication

**Permitted Activities:**
- Provide lesson updates and progress reports to students and parents
- Answer questions about chess instruction and class materials
- Discuss student performance, challenges, and recommendations for improvement
- Request feedback about lesson quality
- Schedule and confirm lesson times through authorized academy channels

**Prohibited Activities:**
- Do not negotiate or change pricing independently
- Do not promise refunds, discounts, or special arrangements without management approval
- Do not provide coaching services outside contracted channels
- Do not share personal contact information (phone, email, address, social media) for lessons
- Do not conduct private business dealings directly with customers

### 3.2 Social Media and Online Conduct

- Do not add students or parents to personal social media accounts
- Do not discuss student performance on public platforms
- Do not make negative comments about the academy, management, or other coaches online
- Do not promote personal coaching services or redirect customers
- Do not engage in disputes or arguments with parents/customers online
- All student/customer-related posts require management approval

### 3.3 Conflict Resolution

- Address complaints and concerns professionally and calmly
- Escalate serious issues to management immediately
- Do not make unilateral decisions about student placement, promotion, or removal
- Work collaboratively with parents and management to resolve disputes
- Document all significant customer interactions

---

## 4. Private Lessons Guidelines

### 4.1 Authorized Private Lessons

Private lessons are only permitted when:
- Scheduled through the Jess Academy system or with explicit management approval
- Conducted at designated locations (academy facilities, school partner sites, or approved venues)
- Priced according to academy's standard rate card
- Properly logged in the academy's CRM system
- Documented for student progress tracking

### 4.2 Prohibited Private Arrangements

Coaches are **strictly prohibited** from:
- Offering personal/independent coaching to academy customers outside of academy channels
- Collecting payment directly from students or parents for any academy-related instruction
- Using academy's student list to solicit private clients
- Charging different rates than academy-approved pricing
- Conducting lessons without recording them in the academy system
- Teaching academy curriculum to customers through competing organizations

### 4.3 Student Continuity

- If a student requests private lessons, refer them to management
- Do not negotiate directly with parents about taking private students
- Do not offer guarantees or promises outside of academy-approved lesson plans
- All private lesson arrangements must be formalized through the academy

---

## 5. Marketing Schools & Channel Guidelines

### 5.1 Professional Representation

When representing Jess Academy at partner schools or through marketed channels:
- Maintain consistent pricing and terms across all venues
- Do not make unauthorized promises or commitments to schools
- Follow the academy's approved curriculum and teaching methods
- Report all attendance and enrollment data accurately
- Notify management of any partnership issues or concerns immediately

### 5.2 Student Recruitment

- Do not recruit students away from academy programs to private lessons
- Do not disparage academy's programs to recruit students to your own services
- Do not share proprietary academy materials with school partners without approval
- All student recruitment follows academy's approved marketing materials

### 5.3 Program Integrity

- Deliver the full scope of services promised to each marketing school or channel
- Do not reduce class quality or material scope
- Maintain attendance records and report them accurately
- Do not oversubscribe classes beyond capacity
- Communicate program changes to management and customers with adequate notice

---

## 6. Financial & Payment Obligations

### 6.1 Compensation

- Accept only compensation authorized by the academy
- Do not accept tips or gifts that could influence objectivity
- Do not charge students directly for any academy-related services
- Report all compensation arrangements to management
- Ensure all invoicing goes through official academy channels

### 6.2 Prohibited Financial Activities

Coaches are prohibited from:
- Collecting fees directly from parents or students
- Accepting payment for lessons in cash without documentation
- Creating unauthorized payment plans or financing arrangements
- Sharing commission structures or revenue details with students
- Offering discounts or special rates without explicit approval

---

## 7. Data & CRM System Requirements

### 7.1 System Compliance

- Accurately log all classes, attendance, and student progress in the CRM
- Record honest assessments of student understanding and readiness
- Do not falsify attendance records or progress reports
- Update student information promptly
- Keep all login credentials secure and confidential

### 7.2 Promotion Requests

- Submit promotion requests only for students who genuinely meet criteria
- Provide clear justification for promotion recommendations
- Do not pressure management to approve questionable promotions
- Accept promotion decisions made by management

### 7.3 Schedule Management

- Request schedule changes through authorized channels only
- Provide adequate notice for any requested changes
- Fulfill all confirmed schedule commitments
- Notify management immediately of any availability changes

---

## 8. Prohibited Activities

Coaches are **strictly prohibited** from:

### 8.1 Competition Activities
- Running competing coaching services to academy students
- Recruiting academy students to alternative programs you operate
- Using academy's brand, materials, or reputation to promote competing services
- Offering the same curriculum or instruction to competing organizations

### 8.2 Unethical Conduct
- Accepting payments under the table or off-record
- Engaging in any form of discrimination or harassment
- Creating unsafe or inappropriate learning environments
- Showing favoritism that affects student advancement
- Using academy platform for personal business promotion

### 8.3 Violation of Trust
- Sharing confidential academy or student information
- Accessing CRM data beyond your assigned role
- Modifying or falsifying student records
- Using student data for personal gain or research

### 8.4 Improper Student Relationships
- Developing inappropriate personal relationships with students
- Using coaching position to gain personal advantage
- Engaging in any form of exploitation or coercion
- Conducting unauthorized off-site interactions with students

---

## 9. Student Promotion & Assessment

### 9.1 Fair Evaluation Standards

- Assess students based solely on chess skill and understanding
- Use consistent criteria for all students in the same tier
- Document reasons for recommendations
- Avoid bias based on payment status, family connections, or personal preference

### 9.2 Promotion Process

- Recommend promotions through the CRM system
- Management makes final promotion decisions
- Do not bypass the official promotion process
- Communicate promotion decisions professionally to students and parents

---

## 10. Health, Safety & Safeguarding

### 10.1 Student Safety

- Maintain a safe, respectful learning environment
- Report any safety concerns or incidents immediately
- Follow all academy safeguarding policies
- Never conduct lessons alone with a student under age 18 if you are an adult (outside academy-supervised settings)
- Maintain appropriate physical boundaries with all students

### 10.2 Incident Reporting

- Report any student incidents, injuries, or concerns to management
- Document incidents accurately in the CRM system
- Cooperate fully with any investigations or inquiries
- Do not retaliate against students or parents who report concerns

---

## 11. Compliance & Enforcement

### 11.1 Policy Agreement

By accepting a coaching position at Jess Academy, you acknowledge:
- You have read and understood this policy
- You agree to comply with all guidelines
- You understand violations may result in disciplinary action
- You commit to upholding the academy's standards and reputation

### 11.2 Violations & Consequences

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Written warning
- Suspension from teaching duties
- Reduction in assigned classes or sessions
- Termination of coaching contract
- Legal action for breach of contract or fiduciary duty
- Referral to appropriate authorities if laws are violated

### 11.3 Investigation Process

Any reported violation will be:
- Investigated fairly and thoroughly
- Documented in writing
- Discussed with the coach involved
- Escalated to management as appropriate
- Resolved according to the severity of the violation

---

## 12. Dispute Resolution

### 12.1 Raising Concerns

If you have questions about this policy or need clarification:
- Contact management directly
- Request a formal policy review meeting
- Document your concern in writing
- Allow management time to respond

### 12.2 Grievance Process

If you disagree with a management decision:
1. Request a meeting to discuss concerns
2. Present your perspective and supporting documentation
3. Discuss potential resolutions
4. Management will provide a written response within 5 business days
5. If unresolved, escalate to senior management
