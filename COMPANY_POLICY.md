## 1. Mission & Values

### 1.1 Company Mission

Kingz Chess Academy is dedicated to developing chess excellence and critical thinking skills in students of all ages and levels, creating a supportive community of learners, and fostering a lifelong passion for the game of chess.

### 1.2 Core Values

- **Excellence** - We maintain the highest standards in instruction and customer service
- **Integrity** - We operate with honesty, transparency, and ethical business practices
- **Inclusivity** - We welcome students from all backgrounds and skill levels
- **Innovation** - We continually improve our programs and teaching methods
- **Community** - We build strong relationships with students, families, and partners
- **Accountability** - We take responsibility for our actions and outcomes

## 3. Employment Standards

### 3.1 Equal Opportunity Employment

Kingz Chess Academy is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate based on:
- Race, color, or national origin
- Gender, gender identity, or sexual orientation
- Age, disability, or veteran status
- Religion or political belief
- Any other legally protected characteristic

### 3.2 At-Will Employment

Employment with Kingz Chess Academy is at-will, meaning:
- Either the company or the employee can terminate employment at any time
- Termination may be with or without cause
- This does not waive any legal protections or contractual agreements

### 3.3 Independent Contractors vs. Employees

- **Coaches** may be classified as independent contractors or employees based on work arrangement
- Classification follows IRS guidelines and state labor law
- Tax documentation and compliance requirements apply to each classification

### 3.4 Background Checks

All coaching and student-facing staff:
- Must pass a background check before beginning work
- Must disclose any criminal history or legal proceedings
- Must maintain clean background status while employed
- Failure to disclose prior offenses is grounds for immediate termination

## 4. Attendance & Scheduling

### 4.1 Scheduling Requirements

All coaches and staff must:
- Confirm their availability regularly through the CRM system
- Notify management of schedule changes with adequate notice (minimum 48 hours)
- Arrive 15 minutes early to prepare for classes
- Fulfill all confirmed commitments
- Maintain consistent scheduling for regular students

### 4.2 Attendance Policy

- Attendance is critical to student success and academy reputation
- Unexcused absences will result in disciplinary action
- If unable to attend, notify management immediately
- Three unexcused absences may result in termination
- Excessive tardiness is grounds for disciplinary action

### 4.3 Cancellations & Rescheduling

- Cancellations must be approved by management
- Students must be notified at least 48 hours in advance
- Rescheduling requires coordination through the academy system
- No unauthorized lesson cancellations are permitted

### 4.4 Vacation & Time Off

- Vacation requests must be submitted in writing at least 30 days in advance
- Requests are approved based on operational needs
- Substitutes must be arranged for all scheduled commitments
- Compensation during vacation is determined by employment classification

## 5. Compensation & Benefits

### 5.1 Payment Structure

**Coaching Compensation:**
- Rates are determined by experience level, qualifications, and position
- Payment is made according to the agreed-upon contract
- Compensation may include per-class fees, hourly rates, or salary
- Independent contractors receive 1099 documentation
- Employees receive W-2 documentation and payroll taxes are withheld

### 5.2 Payment Schedule

- Employees are paid bi-weekly via direct deposit
- Independent contractors are invoiced monthly for services rendered
- All invoices must be submitted with detailed lesson logs
- Payment is processed within 30 days of invoice submission

### 5.3 Benefits (Employees Only)

Eligibility and benefits vary by employment classification:
- Health insurance options may be available
- Retirement plan access (if applicable)
- Paid time off according to state law and company policy
- Professional development opportunities
- Employee discount on academy services (10%)

### 5.4 Independent Contractor Terms

- No company benefits or payroll deductions
- Responsible for self-employment taxes (estimated quarterly)
- Must maintain professional liability insurance
- Eligible for 1099 income documentation
- Contract terms are outlined in separate agreement

### 5.5 Expense Reimbursement

- Pre-approved business expenses can be reimbursed
- Receipts must be submitted with reimbursement requests
- Reimbursement is processed within 30 days
- Personal expenses are not reimbursable

## 6. Code of Conduct

### 6.1 Professional Standards

All staff must:
- Treat students, parents, and colleagues with respect and dignity
- Maintain professional appearance and demeanor
- Communicate clearly and honestly
- Arrive prepared and organized
- Model ethical behavior and integrity
- Uphold the academy's reputation at all times

### 6.2 Workplace Respect & Harassment Policy

**Zero Tolerance for:**
- Sexual harassment or unwanted advances
- Discrimination based on protected characteristics
- Bullying, intimidation, or hostile behavior
- Verbal abuse or disrespectful language
- Retaliation against those reporting violations

**Reporting:**
- Report harassment immediately to management
- All reports are investigated confidentially
- Retaliation against reporters is prohibited
- Corrective action up to termination will be taken

### 6.3 Substance Abuse Policy

- No illegal drugs, alcohol, or intoxicating substances at work
- Coaches must not teach while under the influence
- Violations result in immediate disciplinary action
- Substance abuse treatment resources are available

### 6.4 Social Media & Online Conduct

- Do not post negative comments about the company or colleagues
- Do not share academy business details on personal social media
- Do not connect with students on personal social media
- Do not engage in disputes with customers online
- Violations of social media policy may result in discipline

### 6.5 Conflict of Interest

Staff must disclose:
- Any business relationship with academy customers
- Competing coaching or instruction services
- Financial interests that may conflict with company interests
- Close personal relationships with colleagues in supervisory roles

Conflicts of interest must be managed to protect academy operations.

## 7. Confidentiality & Intellectual Property

### 7.1 Confidential Information

Confidential information includes:
- Student names, contact information, and progress records
- Pricing, commission structures, and financial data
- Proprietary lesson plans and teaching materials
- Customer lists and marketing strategies
- Employee compensation and personal information
- Partnership agreements and contracts

### 7.2 Non-Disclosure Obligations

All staff must:
- Keep confidential information secure
- Not discuss academy business with unauthorized parties
- Not share student data with third parties
- Not disclose pricing or financial information
- Not use academy data for personal benefit
- Maintain confidentiality even after employment ends

### 7.3 Intellectual Property Rights

- All lesson plans, materials, and content created for the academy are academy property
- Staff may not use academy materials for competing services
- Academy materials cannot be shared without written permission
- Staff members grant the academy non-exclusive rights to their teaching methods
- Original improvements to academy methods may be acknowledged

### 7.4 Breach of Confidentiality

Violations result in:
- Written warning for first offense
- Suspension for repeated violations
- Termination for serious breaches
- Legal action for damages if applicable

## 8. Health, Safety & Security

### 8.1 Safe Environment

The academy is committed to:
- Maintaining clean, safe facilities
- Ensuring proper equipment maintenance
- Preventing harassment and violence
- Following all health codes and regulations
- Protecting student and staff well-being

### 8.2 Emergency Procedures

- All staff must know emergency exit routes
- Report hazards or safety concerns immediately
- Follow emergency protocols (evacuation, lockdown, etc.)
- Complete emergency drills annually
- Maintain emergency contact information current

### 8.3 Injury Reporting

- Report all injuries immediately, no matter how minor
- Seek medical attention if needed
- Complete incident documentation
- Cooperate with any investigations
- Maintain workplace safety standards

### 8.4 Cybersecurity & Data Protection

- Protect login credentials and never share passwords
- Report security breaches immediately
- Use approved devices for accessing academy systems
- Do not install unauthorized software
- Comply with data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)

### 8.5 Safeguarding Policies

**Student Protection:**
- Never be alone with a student in closed spaces (unless approved exception)
- Maintain appropriate professional boundaries
- Report any concerns about student safety
- Follow child protection protocols
- Complete safeguarding training annually

## 9. Performance Management

### 9.1 Performance Expectations

Staff are expected to:
- Meet all scheduled commitments
- Maintain accurate records in the CRM system
- Provide quality instruction and customer service
- Continuously improve teaching methods
- Respond professionally to feedback
- Meet academy performance standards

### 9.2 Performance Reviews

- Annual reviews are conducted for all staff
- Reviews assess performance against stated goals
- Feedback is provided on strengths and areas for improvement
- Goals are set for the upcoming review period
- Review results may affect compensation or advancement

### 9.3 Improvement Plans

For performance concerns:
1. Issue is identified and discussed with staff member
2. Written improvement plan is created with specific goals
3. Timeline for improvement is established (typically 30-90 days)
4. Progress is monitored through regular check-ins
5. Continuation or termination is determined at review point

### 9.4 Positive Recognition

- Excellent performance is recognized and rewarded
- Staff member of the month recognition programs
- Performance bonuses may be awarded for exceptional results
- Advancement opportunities for high performers
- Professional development funding for top staff

## 10. Termination & Separation

### 10.1 Grounds for Immediate Termination

Conduct resulting in immediate termination without severance:
- Theft or dishonesty
- Illegal activity or criminal conduct
- Violence or threats
- Serious safety violations
- Sexual harassment or abuse
- Falsifying records or student data
- Breach of confidentiality
- Being under the influence at work
- Repeated policy violations despite warnings

### 10.2 Grounds for Cause Termination

Grounds for termination with notice:
- Repeated attendance problems
- Failure to meet performance standards
- Insubordination or refusal to follow policies
- Unprofessional conduct
- Negligence in duties
- Unauthorized absence

### 10.3 Voluntary Resignation

- Provide at least two weeks' written notice
- Complete knowledge transfer and documentation
- Return all academy property and materials
- Maintain confidentiality after departure
- Cooperate with transition process

### 10.4 Final Paycheck

- Final paycheck includes all earned compensation
- Deductions are made for unreturned equipment or materials
- Timing follows state law requirements
- All benefits are explained in final separation meeting

### 10.5 References & Recommendations

- Reference requests are handled by management only
- Provide factual information about employment
- Verification limited to dates and positions held
- Recommend leaving detailed inquiries to HR

## 11. Dispute Resolution

### 11.1 Informal Resolution

For concerns or disputes:
1. Discuss issue directly with immediate supervisor
2. Allow reasonable time for response (5 business days)
3. Document the concern in writing
4. Request written response from management

### 11.2 Formal Grievance Process

If informal resolution fails:
1. Submit formal written grievance to management
2. Include specific dates, facts, and impact
3. Clearly state desired resolution
4. Management conducts investigation (within 10 business days)
5. Written response provided with decision and reasoning
6. Appeal process available if unsatisfied

### 11.3 Mediation

- Disputes may be subject to mediation
- Neutral third-party mediator selected by mutual agreement
- Both parties present perspective and work toward resolution
- Mediation is confidential and non-binding

### 11.4 No Retaliation

- Retaliation for raising concerns is prohibited
- Protected reporting includes safety, legal, and ethical issues
- Retaliation complaints are investigated immediately
- Severe consequences for retaliatory conduct
